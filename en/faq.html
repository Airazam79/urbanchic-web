Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on +34 951 196 922 or email info@living4malaga.com.

Where is Urban Chic Apartments?

At Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9, 29005 Málaga, facing the square of the same name in the old town. The Atarazanas Market is 2 minutes away on foot, Calle Larios 6 and Plaza de la Constitución 9.

How many people can stay?

Urban Chic 1, 2A and 3A have one bedroom with a double bed and a double sofa bed in the living room: up to 4 guests. Urban Chic 2B and 3B have two bedrooms (a double and a twin), two bathrooms and a sofa bed: up to 6. Urban Penthouse Terrace has three bedrooms (two doubles and a single), two bathrooms and a sofa bed: up to 6.

Which apartment has a terrace?

Urban Penthouse Terrace, the fourth-floor penthouse: a 15 m² private terrace with artificial grass, a seating area, sun loungers and an awning that covers it completely. The other five have a French balcony over the square.

Is there a lift?

Yes.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

How do I get in?

With an electronic lock. Once you complete online check-in we send you the access instructions, so there's no need to meet anyone for keys.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes, every apartment has a kitchen with oven, microwave, fridge-freezer, coffee maker, toaster, kettle, blender and a washing machine. In Urban Chic 1 the kitchen is open-plan; in the others it's separate. Only the penthouse has a dishwasher.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, all apartments have air conditioning and heating.

Can I park nearby?

The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot, and the Avenida de Andalucía one 4.

How do I get here from the airport?

Take the C1 local train to Málaga-Centro Alameda (about 12 minutes), then walk for 6 minutes. For taxis, see the official fares in the airport guide; taxis can't stop right outside the door, but they can at the entrance of the H10 hotel, on the same square.

How do I pay?

30% is paid when you book, and four days before arrival you'll get an email to complete full payment of the booking.

Is cleaning or a towel change included?

There are always clean sheets and one set of towels per guest, and extra towels are available for an additional charge. Stays of up to 7 nights don't include cleaning or a change of towels and sheets. For 8 to 12 nights we leave spare bed linen and towels for you to change. From 13 nights we come once a week to change them.

Can I cancel?

Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 5 days before arrival; after that, the 30% paid when booking is not refunded. Non-refundable rates allow no date changes, and cancelling them costs 100% of the stay. For stays of more than a month, you need to cancel at least one month before arrival.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, €300 for any incidents caused by guests during the stay.

What is the area like?

It's a lively area right in the old town, surrounded by cafés, restaurants and shops. The apartments face the square and street noise can be heard inside.

Who manages the apartments?

Living4Malaga, a Málaga company specialising in holiday rentals. The building is listed in the Andalusian Tourism Register under number A/MA/02076.

Where do I book?

On living4malaga.com, the management company's official website. Each apartment page takes you straight to its booking page.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. The direct rate is always the lowest, because it carries no intermediary commissions; the difference varies by date.
