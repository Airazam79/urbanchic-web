A day from Hoyo de Esparteros

A full day on foot, stop by stop, with the real walking time of each leg. Swipe or use the arrows.

  1. Morning

    Atarazanas Market

    2 minutes from the door. Fruit and the day's catch under the great stained-glass window.

  2. Late morning

    The cathedral

    7 minutes from the market. Its missing tower earned it the nickname ‘La Manquita’, the one-armed lady.

  3. Midday

    Picasso Museum

    4 minutes from the cathedral, in the Buenavista Palace. His birthplace is on Plaza de la Merced, a little further up.

  4. Afternoon

    Roman Theatre and Alcazaba

    4 minutes from the museum. A 1st-century BC theatre and a Moorish fortress, side by side at the foot of Gibralfaro hill.

  5. Sunset

    Muelle Uno

    11 minutes from the Alcazaba. The harbour promenade, the Centre Pompidou's coloured cube and the last light of the day on the boats.

  6. Night

    Back to Hoyo de Esparteros

    14 minutes from the harbour. If you're staying in the penthouse, the terrace is waiting.

What people ask us most

Where is Urban Chic Apartments?

At Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9, 29005 Málaga, facing the square of the same name in the old town. The Atarazanas Market is 2 minutes away on foot, Calle Larios 6 and Plaza de la Constitución 9.

How many people can stay?

Urban Chic 1, 2A and 3A have one bedroom with a double bed and a double sofa bed in the living room: up to 4 guests. Urban Chic 2B and 3B have two bedrooms (a double and a twin), two bathrooms and a sofa bed: up to 6. Urban Penthouse Terrace has three bedrooms (two doubles and a single), two bathrooms and a sofa bed: up to 6.

Which apartment has a terrace?

Urban Penthouse Terrace, the fourth-floor penthouse: a 15 m² private terrace with artificial grass, a seating area, sun loungers and an awning that covers it completely. The other five have a French balcony over the square.

Is there a lift?

Yes.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

How do I get in?

With an electronic lock. Once you complete online check-in we send you the access instructions, so there's no need to meet anyone for keys.
