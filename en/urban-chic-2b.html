The apartment

Urban Chic 2B is on the second floor, facing the square and next to the Atarazanas Market. It's bright, with contemporary decor, elegant touches and natural materials.

It has a separate, equipped kitchen and a living and dining room with a sofa bed, a dining table and a TV. The bedrooms are off the hallway: the main one with a double bed and en-suite shower room, and a second with two single beds. There's a second shower room, and air conditioning and heating in the bedrooms and living room.
