A penthouse with a private terrace in Málaga old town

Urban Penthouse Terrace, for six, at Hoyo de Esparteros 9

Urban Penthouse Terrace is Urban Chic's top-floor apartment: three bedrooms, two bathrooms and room for six, with a 15 m² private terrace with artificial grass, a seating area, sun loungers and an awning that covers it completely. It's on the fourth floor of a building with a lift at Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9, a 2-minute walk from the Atarazanas Market and 6 minutes from Calle Larios.

Questions about the penthouse with a terrace

Which Urban Chic apartment has a private terrace?

Only Urban Penthouse Terrace, the fourth-floor penthouse. The other five have a French balcony over Plaza de Hoyo de Esparteros.

What is the terrace like?

It's 15 m², with artificial grass, a seating area, sun loungers and an awning that covers it completely.

Is the terrace private?

Yes. Only the guests staying in the penthouse use it.

How do you get out to the terrace?

From the living room and from one of the double bedrooms.

How many people can stay in the penthouse?

Up to six: it has two double bedrooms, a third with a single bed, a sofa bed in the living room and two bathrooms.

Is there a lift?

Yes, the building has a lift.

Is there a dishwasher?

Yes. The penthouse is the only apartment in the building with a dishwasher.

What if the penthouse isn't available?

Urban Chic 2B and 3B also sleep six, with two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a sofa bed. They have no terrace, but a French balcony over the square.

Can you hear the street?

Yes. The building is right in the old town, facing the square, and street noise can be heard inside and on the terrace.

Can I park nearby?

The Camas and Avenida de Andalucía public car parks are both a 4-minute walk away.
