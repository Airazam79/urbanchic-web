The apartment

Urban Chic 1 is on the first floor of Hoyo de Esparteros 9, facing the square and a few steps from the Atarazanas Market.

The heart of the apartment is a living and dining room with a fully equipped open-plan kitchen. The bedroom, with a double bed, has a balcony over the square, and the recently renovated bathroom off the hallway has a large walk-in shower. A double sofa bed in the living room sleeps two more.

Before you book

Payment
30% is paid when you book. Four days before arrival you'll get an email to complete full payment of the booking.
Check-in
With an electronic lock, from 16:00. Earlier access isn't possible. Check-out by 12:00.
Linen and cleaning
There are always clean sheets and one set of towels per guest; extra towels are available for an additional charge. Stays of up to 7 nights don't include cleaning or a change of towels and sheets. For 8 to 12 nights we leave spare bed linen and towels for you to change. From 13 nights we come once a week to change them.
Deposit
€300 for any incidents caused by guests during the stay.
Cancellation
Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 5 days before arrival; after that, the 30% paid when booking is not refunded. Non-refundable rates allow no date changes, and cancelling them costs 100% of the stay. For stays of more than a month, you need to cancel at least one month before arrival.
