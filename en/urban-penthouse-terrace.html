The apartment

Urban Penthouse Terrace is the top-floor apartment, on the fourth floor, facing the square and next to the Atarazanas Market. It's spacious and bright, with boho-style decor and patterned wallpaper.

Its highlight is a 15 m² private terrace with artificial grass, a seating area, sun loungers and an awning that covers it completely. You step out onto it from the living room and from one of the bedrooms.

Inside there's a large, equipped separate kitchen with a dishwasher; a living and dining room with a sofa bed, a table for six and a TV; and three bedrooms off the hallway: the main one with a double bed and en-suite shower room, another double that opens onto the terrace, and one with a single bed. There's a second shower room, air conditioning and heating in the bedrooms and living room, and a split unit in the kitchen.
