Parking near Hoyo de Esparteros, in Málaga old town

Public car parks within walking distance, and how to drive into the centre without a fine

The nearest public car parks to Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9 are SMASSA's Camas and Avenida de Andalucía, both a 4-minute walk away. On weekdays they cost €2.09 for the first hour and €25.34 for 24 hours, VAT included. Access to the old town is camera-controlled: if your car park is inside that zone, drive straight to it.

Driving in without a fine

  • Outside the restricted zone

    Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9 and the Camas and Avenida de Andalucía car parks are outside the old town's Special Protection Zone: you can drive there without authorisation.

  • Camera-controlled old town

    Only authorised vehicles, or those with a registered declaration such as residents' cars, may enter the old town's Special Protection Zone. Drivers heading to a public car park inside the zone may enter too, and the car park registers the number plate. Entering without authorisation carries a €200 fine.

  • Drive straight there

    If the car park is inside that zone, you can't stop or park anywhere else on the way in or out.

  • If it's full

    If you head to a car park inside the zone and it's full, ask the staff to log your number plate before you leave.

  • Low Emission Zone

    The High Court of Andalusia struck down the Low Emission Zone articles of the Mobility Bylaw. Since 14 October 2026 the City Council has stopped fining drivers for entering or driving in it. On 19 October 2026 the ruling was not yet final. According to the City Council, the ruling only strikes down the LEZ articles. The access restrictions for the old town (art. 12 of the Bylaw and the decree of May 2025) still apply.

  • No stopping at the door

    Neither cars nor taxis can stop outside the front door: you have to stop nearby and walk the last stretch with your luggage.

Sources: Málaga Mobility Bylaw (Official Gazette no. 231, 29/11/2024), Special Protection Zones decree (Official Gazette no. 91, 15/05/2025) and City Council note of 19/10/2026 on the Low Emission Zone ruling. Questions about driving

Where can I park near Hoyo de Esparteros?

The nearest public car parks are SMASSA's Camas and Avenida de Andalucía, both a 4-minute walk away. Plaza de la Marina (7 minutes) and Tejón y Rodríguez (12 minutes) are also within walking distance.

Will my van or tall car fit?

It depends on its height. Avenida de Andalucía takes up to 2.30 m, Camas up to 2.20 m, and Plaza de la Marina and Tejón y Rodríguez up to 2.00 m, according to SMASSA's car park pages.

Can I be fined for driving into the old town?

You don't need to enter it to get here: the building and the Camas and Avenida de Andalucía car parks are outside the restricted zone. You can be fined if you enter the Special Protection Zone without authorisation and aren't going straight to a public car park inside it. The car park registers your plate; if it's full, ask the staff to log it before you leave. The fine is €200.

Will I be fined for entering the Low Emission Zone?

Since 14 October 2026, no. The High Court of Andalusia struck down the Low Emission Zone articles of the Mobility Bylaw and the City Council stopped issuing fines. According to the City Council, the ruling only strikes down the LEZ articles. The access restrictions for the old town (art. 12 of the Bylaw and the decree of May 2025) still apply.

How much does parking cost?

At Camas, Avenida de Andalucía and Tejón y Rodríguez on weekdays, €2.09 for the first hour and €25.34 for 24 hours, VAT included. On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, and every day at Plaza de la Marina, €2.39 an hour and €28.03 for 24 hours. You pay by the minute. These are the official fares of SMASSA, the municipal company that runs these car parks (Official Gazette no. 241 of 2021, frozen in 2026). This is for guidance only: Urban Chic doesn't run the car parks or charge for them. Check the price at the pay station or in the app.

Can I stop outside the door to unload?

No. Neither cars nor taxis can stop outside the front door: you have to stop nearby.

Is there a lift for luggage?

Yes, the building has a lift.
