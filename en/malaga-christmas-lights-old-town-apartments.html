Christmas in Málaga, 6 minutes from the Calle Larios lights

See the lights and walk back home

The Calle Larios Christmas lights are a 6-minute walk from Urban Chic at Calle Hoyo de Esparteros 9. In 2025 the switch-on ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30, and the light and music show ran three times a day until 4 January, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00; on 24 and 31 December the lights were on but without music. The City Council has not yet confirmed the 2026 dates.

Christmas in the old town, on foot

Walking minutes from Hoyo de Esparteros 9, with what was on over Christmas 2025-26:

  • 2 min

    Atarazanas Market

    The route of the Three Kings parade on 5 January 2026 included Calle Atarazanas, the market's street, and Calle Puerta del Mar.

  • 6 min

    Calle Larios

    The ‘Natividad de Luz’ light and music show, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00 until 4 January. On 24 and 31 December, lights without music.

  • 9 min

    Plaza de la Constitución

    ‘Música en Navidad’ concerts from 20 to 29 December (except the 24th) and, on New Year's Eve, the chimes with free grapes and party favours from 21:30 to 23:45 or while stocks last.

  • 9 min

    The cathedral

    The ‘El Pescador de Sueños’ projection show on the tower, at 19:00, 20:30 and 22:00, until 4 January (except 24 and 31 December).

  • 14 min

    Muelle Uno

    The harbour promenade, for a walk between shows.

Dates to note

  • Switch-on

    In 2025 the ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30. According to idealista (article of 5/11/2026), the switch-on would be on Friday 27 November at 19:00; the City Council has not confirmed it.

  • Lighting hours

    In 2025-26 the lights were on from 18:30 to 00:00 Sunday to Wednesday, until 02:00 Thursday to Saturday and on the eve of public holidays, and until 06:00 on the nights of 24 and 31 December.

  • Municipal Nativity scene

    In 2025-26 it was free to visit from its opening on the evening of 5 December until 6 January, at City Hall: 10:00 to 14:00 and 17:00 to 21:00 (on 8 December, 10:00 to 14:00 and 18:30 to 21:00), with over 400 figures.

  • With children

    On 22 December 2025 there was a children's puppet show in Plaza Enrique García Herrera (Camas), also home to the Kings' letterbox for the city-centre district. From 2 to 4 January, the royal envoy and postman were in Plaza de la Constitución from 19:00 to 20:00.

  • Public holidays

    In Andalusia: Monday 7 (moved from the 6th), Tuesday 8 and Friday 25 December 2026, and Friday 1 and Wednesday 6 January 2027.

Practical tips

  • Crowds on Calle Larios

    In 2025 the local police planned over 1,300 extra shifts across the Christmas period, including crowd control on and around Calle Larios.

  • Traffic

    In 2025, during the Calle Larios shows, the Alameda Principal or Paseo del Parque could be briefly closed to private traffic.

  • Getting here

    Málaga-Centro Alameda station, on the C1 line to the airport, is 6 minutes away on foot, and the Camas public car park 4.

  • Climate

    All the apartments have heating and air conditioning.

Questions about Christmas at Hoyo de Esparteros

When are the Calle Larios lights switched on in 2026?

According to idealista (article of 5/11/2026), the switch-on would be on Friday 27 November at 19:00; the City Council has not confirmed it. In 2025 the switch-on ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30.

How far are the Calle Larios lights?

Calle Larios is a 6-minute walk from Hoyo de Esparteros 9, Plaza de la Constitución 9 minutes and the cathedral 9.

What time is the light and music show?

Over Christmas 2025-26 there were three shows a day until 4 January, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00. On 24 and 31 December the lights were on, but without music. The 2026 times have not been published yet.

Which apartment suits a group Christmas dinner?

The penthouse, Urban Penthouse Terrace: it sleeps six and has a large kitchen, a table for six and the building's only dishwasher. Urban Chic 3B also sleeps six, with two bathrooms.

Is there a lift?

Yes, the building has a lift. The penthouse is on the fourth floor.

Which days are public holidays?

In Andalusia: 7, 8 and 25 December 2026, and 1 and 6 January 2027.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. It carries no intermediary commissions; the difference varies by date.
